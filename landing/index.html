TECHNOLOGY

Anonymized at the Source.
Judged on the Edge.

Faces never leave the sensor. GRADIS replaces every person with a rigged 3D mannequin that mirrors their pose in real time, then lets AI reason about the scene — not the identity.

01

GRADIS-25 Mannequin Rig

17 detected joints extended with a derived spine line and hand & foot endpoints — precise enough to read a raised arm, a collapsing torso, or a body over a ledge.

02

Occlusion-Aware Tracking

Identity-stable multi-person tracking with mannequin reconstruction through crowds, trees, and partial views. Aggressor and victim never swap.

03

On-Device Scene Judgment

An embedded vision-language model assesses the redacted scene — context, posture, environment — and escalates only credible risk. No cloud round-trips. No raw video uploads.
